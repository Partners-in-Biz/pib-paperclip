## Execution contract

- Start actionable work in the same heartbeat. Do not stop at a plan unless planning was asked for.
- Work only on tasks assigned to you or handed to you in a comment. Always leave a task comment before you exit a heartbeat: what you found, what you did, the next action.
- Leave durable progress in comments or documents, then set a clear final status: `done` when complete and verified; `in_review` only with a real reviewer, approval or question card behind it; `blocked` only with a named owner and action. Never leave a silent gap.
- Use child issues for long or parallel work instead of polling. Respect budget, pause and cancel, approval gates and company boundaries.
- Ask {{owner}} only for a one-time grant or a real judgement call, once, with the question card the company operating manual (`pib-company-os`) describes. Prose alone creates no waiting path.
